TECHNICIAN

I am a former Arcade Techncian Manager with over 15 years of of electronics repair experience. I've trained technicians and worked myself to repair hundreds of different games, registers, kiosks, jukeboxes, audio equipment, as well as PC and general electronics. After many years of interest, I purchased my first P.E.V. in 2021. I disassembled my Onewheel pint X within hours of it arriving and began learning all I could about the calibration, upgrading, and repair of my equipment. I started with small cosmetic changes and a new tire. Within a few months I found myself upgrading to VESC and trying every new feature and accessory I could get my hands on. I was hooked. I had purchased the parts and built another larger board by the end of the year. These days I never miss a chance to get out and ride, either walking my dog or racing the trails. Then in 2025, after I was unable to find any official shops still operating in my area, I posted on reddit offering repairs to those in need. The response was incredible. Not only was I able to work on every type of onewheel and vesc based board, assisting riders of all ages, but I found myself working on EUC Esk8 bikes and scooters as well. It feels great being able to apply my technical expertise toward helping so many people. So I am continuing that service through this official shop. Now fully licensed and insured, my hope is that I can reach more people who can benefit from the service I provide. I want to keep as many of these amazing and exhilarating machines working for as long as possible, no matter how you choose to keep riding.
