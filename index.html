TECHNICIAN

I am a former Arcade Technician Manager with over 15 years of electronics repair experience. I've trained technicians and repaired hundreds of different games, registers, kiosks, jukeboxes, audio equipment, PCs and electronics. After many years of interest I purchased my first PEV in 2021. I disassembled my Onewheel Pint X within hours of it arriving and began learning everything I could about calibration, upgrading and repair. Within months I upgraded to VESC and started building a second board. I was hooked. In 2025 I began offering repairs after noticing the lack of local service shops. The response from riders was incredible. Today I service Onewheel, VESC boards, EUC, Esk8, e-bikes and scooters. I never miss a chance to ride, whether I'm walking my dog or flying down trails My goal is simple — keep these exhilarating machines running and riders on the move.
